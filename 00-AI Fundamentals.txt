 		FACE AUTHENTICATION  -  The AI behind Iphone's Face ID
◈═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════◈



Face Authentication is a security system that uses AI & Deep Learning to recognize your face and unlock your iPhone  -  instead of using password or fingerprint.


It maps your face in 3D and learn unique patterns  (like the distance between your eyes or the curve of your jaw)


How Face Authentication Works


Camera Captures face image    

			↓

Infrared Sensor ( In depth scan face in low light )

			↓

Neural Network ( Deep Learning extracts features from face (eyes, nose, geometry ) → Creates face embedding

			↓

Calculate the unique patterns like distance between eyes, curve of the jaw line

			↓

Match Stored Face Data ( Compares face embedding)

			↓

Matched/Rejects (If face data matched , it unlock the iPhone)

✅══════════════════════════════✅
		MACHINE LEARNINGS
✅══════════════════════════════✅


Machine Learning (ML) is a branch (subset) of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that enables computers to learn from data, identify patterns, and make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed for every task.

Simple Definition

Machine Learning is the process of teaching a computer to learn from past data and improve its predictions or decisions over time.




			How Machine Learning Works
--------------------------------------------------

1️⃣ Collect Data → Gather historical data.

2️⃣ Learn Patterns → The ML algorithm analyzes the data and finds relationships.

3️⃣ Build a Model → It creates a model based on those patterns.

4️⃣ Make Predictions → The model predicts outcomes for new data.

5️⃣ Improve → As more data becomes available, the model can be retrained to become more accurate.



Real-Life Examples  ••••••••••••••

Netflix recommends movies based on your watch history.
Amazon suggests products you may like.
Gmail detects spam emails.
Uber/Ola predicts ride fares and demand.
Banks detect fraudulent transactions.
Hospitals help predict diseases from medical data.

HOW AI IS WORKING IN RIDE-HANDLING APPS (OLA, UBER)
◤━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━◥


STEP 1: ABSORB

How many riders are requesting rides?
How many drivers are available?
AI detects factors affecting demand, such as rain, traffic, concerts, or match days.


STEP 2: ANALYZE

AI analyzes past data from similar situations.
It learns how demand, driver availability, and fares changed in those conditions.


STEP 3: PREDICT

Based on current conditions and historical data, AI predicts:
	Ride demand
	Driver availability
	The estimated fare


STEP 4: ACT

AI applies dynamic pricing (surge pricing) when demand is much higher than driver availability.
Higher fares encourage more drivers to become active and help balance supply and demand.


STEP 5: RECHECK

AI continuously monitors demand and driver availability.
As more drivers become available or demand decreases, fares return toward normal.


Simple AI flow:
Observe → Analyze → Predict → Act → Recheck ✅

It is like AI is breathing - prices rise and fall like oxygen, keeping the whole system alive and balanced.
